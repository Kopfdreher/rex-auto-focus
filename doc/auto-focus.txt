*auto-focus.txt*  Resize the focused Rex pane

The focused pane gets `focus` (default 0.70) of the tab. The other panes
share the rest. A tab with one pane is left alone.

    require("auto-focus").setup({ focus = 0.70, threshold = 0.005 })
    require("auto-focus").watch()
    require("auto-focus").apply_session(session_id)
    require("auto-focus").apply_window(session_id, window)

setup({focus}?, {threshold}?)
  Merge options and register the `auto_focus` action when `rex.action`
  exists. `threshold` is how far a split ratio may drift before it is moved.

watch()
  Listen for `session_view_changed` and `active_window_changed`, then size
  every tab whose focused pane or pane count changed. Call this from
  `rex do`, not from `init.lua`.

apply_session({session_id})
  Size the active tab.

apply_window({session_id}, {window})
  Size one window from `rex.session.view`.

rex do auto_focus
  Run the action once on the active tab.
